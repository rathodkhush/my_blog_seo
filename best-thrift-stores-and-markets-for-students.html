Best Places to Thrift on a Student Budget

You do not need a big budget to build a good wardrobe. You need to know where to look. These are the main sources for students.

Street and weekend markets

Large street markets in many Indian cities are known for cheap clothes, and some stalls sell export surplus and secondhand pieces. Go early, bargain politely and check every item carefully.

Thrift stores and charity shops

Search your city name with 'thrift store' or 'preloved store' on maps and social media. Prices are fixed, so you can shop without haggling.

Instagram thrift pages

Many small sellers post new pieces with measurements. Always ask for photos of the label and any flaws, and check return rules before paying.

Clothing swaps

Organise a swap with classmates or hostel friends. Everyone brings five pieces and takes five pieces. It costs nothing.

Family wardrobes

Ask older relatives about jackets, sarees, blazers and shirts they no longer use. Many of these are better made than new fast fashion.
