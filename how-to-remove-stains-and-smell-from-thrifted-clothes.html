How to Remove Stains and Odours from Thrifted Clothes

Most thrifted clothes only need a proper wash. Here is how to deal with smells and stains without damaging the fabric.

First, check the care label

If the label is missing, wash cold by hand and dry in the shade. Heat can set stains and shrink wool.

Musty or stored smell

Soak the piece in cold water with a cup of white vinegar for 30 minutes, then wash as usual. Dry it in open air.

Sweat marks and yellow collars

Make a paste of baking soda and water, rub it on the area, wait 30 minutes and wash. This works best on cotton.

Oil and food stains

Sprinkle baking soda or talc on the stain for a few hours to absorb the oil. Then rub in a little dish soap and wash.

Delicate pieces

For silk, wool and vintage items, test any treatment on a hidden corner first, or take them to a dry cleaner.

Do not mix colours

Wash a new thrift find on its own the first time. Old dyes can bleed.
