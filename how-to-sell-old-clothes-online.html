How to Sell Your Old Clothes Online and Make Money

Selling your own clothes clears space and brings in some money. Here is a simple process.

1. Sort what to sell

Choose clean pieces in good condition. Brands, denim, jackets and ethnic wear tend to sell well.

2. Take clear photos

Use daylight and a plain background. Show the front, back, label and any flaws. Honest photos reduce returns.

3. Write a useful listing

Include size, measurements, fabric, condition and colour. Use words people search, such as 'oversized denim jacket'.

4. Price it fairly

Check what similar items are listed for. Leave a little room for bargaining.

5. Choose where to sell

Instagram pages, Facebook Marketplace, OLX and resale apps are common options in India. Abroad, Depop and Vinted are popular. Read each platform's fees and rules first.

6. Ship and stay safe

Pack items in clean plastic. Prefer tracked shipping or platform payments, and avoid sharing personal bank details in chat.
