How to Spot Good Fabric and Stitching When Thrifting

Price tags in a thrift shop tell you little about quality. These quick checks help you spot pieces that will last.

Read the fibre label

Cotton, wool, linen, silk and denim usually age well. A high percentage of thin polyester can pill and hold smell.

Look at the stitching

Small, even stitches close together are a good sign. Loose threads, skipped stitches and wavy seams are not.

Do the stretch test

Pull a seam gently. If you can see light through the gap, the seam is weak.

Check pattern matching

On checks and stripes, the pattern should line up at the seams and pockets. This usually shows better construction.

Feel the weight

A good tee or shirt has some body. If it feels see-through, it will wear out fast.

Inside out

Turn the piece inside out. Clean, finished edges inside suggest care in the making.
