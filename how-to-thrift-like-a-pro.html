How to Thrift Like a Pro: A Beginner's Guide

Thrifting looks like chaos the first time: packed racks, no sizes, no system. It gets easy once you have a routine. This guide covers the basics.

Go with a short list

Write down three to five things you need, such as a denim jacket, work trousers or a plain white tee. A list stops you from buying something only because it is cheap.

Leave room for one surprise piece. The best finds are often things you did not know you wanted.

Search by fabric, not by size

Sizing in secondhand shops is unreliable because brands and decades differ. Run your hand along the rack and stop when something feels good: thick cotton, denim, wool, linen.

Check the label for the fibre content. Natural fibres usually last longer and are easier to restore than thin polyester.

Try everything on

If there is no trial room, hold the piece against your body and check the shoulders and length. Shoulders are the one part that is hard to alter.

Use the three-wear rule

Before you pay, think of three outfits you can make with the piece using clothes you already own. If you cannot, put it back.
