Vintage vs Thrifted vs Preloved: What Is the Difference?

Sellers use these words differently, and the label can change the price. Here is what each one usually means.

Secondhand

The widest term. It means anything that has had a previous owner.

Thrifted

Secondhand clothing bought at a thrift store or charity shop. Prices are usually low and items are not curated.

Preloved

Secondhand clothes sold by someone who describes them as cared for. Common on resale apps and Instagram pages.

Vintage

Generally, clothing at least 20 years old. True vintage is dated by its style, label and construction, not just by looking old.

Upcycled

Older clothes that have been altered or remade into something new.

Why it matters

Vintage and curated preloved pieces cost more because someone selected and cleaned them. A plain thrift rack is cheaper but needs more of your time.
